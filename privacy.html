Privacy Policy

Vega Astro · Effective September 28, 2026

This policy describes how the Vega Astro app for Android (package com.cmprod.astrology), published by CM Prod, handles information. The app shows zodiac horoscopes and compatibility. It does not create accounts.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your language choice (English, Russian, or Spanish) on the device so it can open in the same language next time. That setting stays in the app’s private storage. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it. We do not receive a copy of this setting.

Information sent when a horoscope loads

Horoscope text is not stored inside the app. When you open a sign, the app downloads a public file from GitHub Pages:

https://cmprod2026-arch.github.io/vega-astro/

The address of the file includes the language and the zodiac sign you opened, for example en/leo.json. The request is a normal HTTPS download. It does not include your name, email, or an account id, because the app has none.

GitHub Pages is operated by GitHub. Like any website, that host can see technical connection data for the request, such as IP address and the file that was requested. That processing is covered by GitHub’s privacy statement. We do not add our own analytics to this request.

Compatibility

Compatibility is calculated on your device from the two signs you pick. The result is not uploaded.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to register or sign in.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not show ads and we do not use an advertising identifier.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not request your location, contacts, photos, or microphone.

Children

Vega Astro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published at this page and the effective date above will change.
